# LEARNING: use the canonical lessons log

`docs/lessons.md` is the only team lessons log. This file describes the workflow and stores no separate instincts.

- Read lessons relevant to the work. Append a short entry when an outcome changes a reusable behavior: date, what happened, lesson, scope and evidence.
- Distinguish verified, reported and inferred evidence; record unknowns. Use numeric confidence only with a documented calibration method.
- Confirm with new evidence. A repetition count can inform review but never automatically makes a rule true.
- Propose a durable rule through the normal reviewer when it has broad, reliable support. Keep the original lesson and link the adopted rule.
- Mark obsolete or contradicted lessons as superseded/disputed with the reason, evidence and review date. Do not automatically delete entries or rules.
- Keep private evidence in approved shared storage; public lessons contain safe summaries and appropriate references.

The three instincts formerly held here are preserved in `docs/lessons.md`; their previous expiry dates are review reminders, not deletion instructions.
