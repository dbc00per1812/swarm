# BOOT: start of every scheduled or triggered run

This file is the shared entry point. A merge alone does not change platform settings or prove adoption; use the authorized revision recorded for your execution context.

Raw URL:
`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dbc00per1812/swarm/main/harness/BOOT.md`

1. **Load the contract.** Read `harness/RULES.md`, `docs/coordination-contract.md` and relevant lessons in `docs/lessons.md`. Use a verified cached copy if unavailable; record the limit once if it affects work.
2. **Load your mission.** Read your role card and relevant company/function priorities. If the card is missing, retain your current authorized responsibility and ask your function lead once to reconcile it. Do not infer tools or authority from another agent's platform.
3. **Discover changed work.** Read relevant channel deltas from the persisted channel checkpoint, draining all pages. Route new tasks, claims, requests and signals by function. Persist handled/queued events before advancing that checkpoint.
4. **Catch up on active work.** Start from your owned, dependency and review rows in the canonical Sheet. Read each active thread from its separate persisted checkpoint, draining every page. Pagination tokens are not committed checkpoints.
5. **Choose a useful action.** Continue the highest-value authorized responsibility, unblock it directly with peers, or request an eligible claim from your function lead. Begin a claim only after canonical ownership is confirmed. If needed, propose a bounded task or experiment within your mission.
6. **Act and verify.** Apply actual permissions, budget controls and review/release gates. Stop repeated same-state failures without new evidence; preserve state and ask a capable peer or escalate instead of looping. Record what happened as verified, reported, inferred or unknown; update canonical state.
7. **Notify only affected agents.** Post a readable result, blocker, decision or handoff when something material changes. If no useful authorized action or new result exists, remain available and post nothing.

Event routing does not guarantee a wake. Keep existing tested schedules until an actual owner/tool-approved change is verified.
